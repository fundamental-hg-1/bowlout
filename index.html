OUR STORY

DEEP BROTHS.
BOLD BOWLS.

At Bowl Out Rice and Noodles, we believe comfort food should hit big on flavor without overcomplicating the experience. Every bowl starts with rich, savory broths, steamed rice, and fresh ingredients prepped daily.

We’re a family-owned kitchen built around doing Asian comfort classics the right way—from slow-braised pork belly miso ramen and crispy chicken karaage to fresh poke and house-made sauces that bring every bite together.

Whether you’re grabbing crispy crab rangoons to share, slurping down a hot bowl of shoyu ramen, or loading up a teriyaki bowl after a busy day in Liberty Township, everything is cooked hot and fresh when you order.

Big portions. House-made sauces. Honest ingredients.
Bold bowls. Done right.
