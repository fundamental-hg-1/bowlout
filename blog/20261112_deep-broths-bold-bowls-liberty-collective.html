Deep Broths & Bold Bowls Have Arrived at Liberty Collective!

Looking for a hot bowl of slow-simmered ramen, crispy chicken karaage, or a loaded rice bowl in Liberty Township? Bowl Out Rice and Noodles is officially open and firing up the woks and broth pots inside Liberty Collective at 6735 Lakota Ln.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick weekday lunch near West Chester and Mason or bringing the family out for dinner, every bowl is prepped fresh and made to order.

What’s on the Lineup

  • Rich, Savory Ramens: Warm up with our Pork Belly Miso Ramen topped with menma, shiitake mushrooms, green onion, and a soft-boiled egg, or dig into our Lemongrass Chicken Shoyu and Pork & Shrimp Wonton Shoyu ramens.
  • Handcrafted Rice Bowls: Choose from crispy Chicken Karaage with spicy hoisin and garlic sriracha aioli, Pork Belly, General Tso’s Chicken, Teriyaki Chicken, Teriyaki Salmon, or a fresh Tuna or Salmon Poke Bowl.
  • Crispy Starters & $9 Kids Meals: Start with Crab Rangoons, Pork Dumplings, or Pork Eggrolls, plus kid-sized bowls of General Chicken, Chicken Shoyu Ramen, or Karaage with Rice.

Hours of Operation

  • Monday – Thursday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Swing by Liberty Collective today or call us at (513) 838-5704—or explore our full ramen and rice bowl menu before you stop in!
